Peak sun hours by city

Peak sun hours (PSH) is the number of full-strength sunlight hours a square metre collects in a day. It is the single input that decides how many panels you need. These are measured values, from NASA POWER daily reanalysis for 2024, for 35 cities.

What peak sun hours means

PSH converts a location's sunlight into the equivalent number of hours at 1,000 W/m². A city at 4.0 PSH collects about 20% less energy per panel than a city at 5.0 PSH over a year. Multiply PSH by panel wattage to get the panel's real daily output before losses.

We report the annual figure as annual irradiance ÷ 365, which is the standard definition and is the number used in the solar panel calculator.
