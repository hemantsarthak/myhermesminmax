How this was calculated

  • Daily load = monthly kWh ÷ 30.
  • Array kW = daily load ÷ (peak sun hours × 0.826 efficiency × roof factor).
  • Panels = array kW ÷ panel wattage, rounded up.
  • Annual output uses each month's real irradiance value.
  • System efficiency follows the NREL PVWatts defaults: 14% system losses and 96% inverter efficiency.

Why city-level data changes the answer

Two cities 300 miles apart can differ by more than 40% in annual solar energy. A calculator that assumes "5 peak sun hours" everywhere oversells panels in the north and undersells them in the desert. These are the real measured values for each city we cover.
